## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an **end-to-end Supply Chain Management Analytics solution** developed using **Microsoft Power BI, DAX, and Jupyter Notebook**.

The dashboard analyzes **66K+ orders** across sales, products, customers, profitability, delivery performance, shipping modes, and geographic regions to provide a comprehensive view of business and supply chain performance.

The goal is to transform raw operational data into an **interactive Business Intelligence solution** that helps identify revenue trends, profitability patterns, product performance, and logistics inefficiencies.

> 💡 **Business Question:**
> How can supply chain and sales data be transformed into actionable insights to improve profitability, product performance, and delivery efficiency?
